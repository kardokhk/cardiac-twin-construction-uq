## Definitions

- ratio = theta_hat / theta* (`ratio_v_t`, `ratio_v_endo`).
- Rows per network: the four primary networks use include_primary rows (both resolutions, RV wall 3 mm). ukbb_cardiac_fcn_sa uses analysis_set ukbb_sensitivity with g1_usable (36 hearts), labelled sensitivity. "expert_1.0" is the expert construction at 1.0 mm (non-reference, include_primary).
- Per heart-network recovery ratio r_hn: median over the 3 theta* and 2 resolutions (6 estimates; 3 for expert_1.0).
- Per heart-network error e_hn: median over the same estimates of |ratio - 1| (primary). Alternative a_hn = |r_hn - 1| is reported beside it (`alt_`, `abserr_med_`).
- Per-heart CV: median over theta* of SD/mean across constructions, ddof 1 (manuscript E1 estimand).
- Cluster bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of hearts with replacement, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261028)` created afresh for each bootstrap call (so all bootstraps over the same heart count use identical resamples), percentile 2.5 and 97.5.

## Item notes

- Item 2: ranks are within heart, 1 = largest e_hn, average ranks for ties; Kendall W with tie correction over the 47 hearts that have all four networks (129, 133, 145 lack one). Friedman chi-squared = m(n - 1)W (matches `scipy.stats.friedmanchisquare`, tested), plus a permutation p-value (2,000 within-heart shuffles, same seed). Under no concordance W is near 1/m (permutation null mean about 0.021), not 0.
- Item 3: heart-network pairs of the four networks (197 pairs, 50 hearts); expert rows are excluded because they have no Dice. Pooled Spearman with the CI from resampling hearts (all pairs of a drawn heart enter). Within-heart Spearman over each heart's 3 or 4 networks, averaged over hearts, CI by resampling hearts. A variant adding ukbb_cardiac is also reported.
- Item 4: networks only, both resolutions, expert excluded; 47 complete hearts for the primary result with CIs; an all-hearts variant (point estimates only, subsets with fewer than two available networks set to missing) is in `results.json`. The k = 4 value (14.0% for v_t) is not E1 (16.1%), because E1 also includes the two expert constructions.
- Item 5: per heart, median over theta* of the SD (ddof 1) of v_t_hat (or v_endo_hat) across the heart's primary constructions, including the reference; a no-reference variant is also given. Velocities are in m/s (fibre velocity fixed at 0.6 m/s).
- Item 6: run time is `grid_wall_median_s` in `results/2026-10-28_armC/constructions.csv`, the median over the 81 grid eikonal runs (9 x 9 theta grid) of a construction of per-run wall time, one thread per run with runs packed on a node (`code/full/calib/run_armC.py`). Refinement-run wall times from `refinement.csv` (rounds 1 and 2) are given beside it. No eikonal timing was found in `results/2026-10-28_known_answer` for the ACDC hearts; that README reports heart 09 of the pilot only.

## Assumptions

1. e_hn (median of |ratio - 1|) is the primary error; a_hn is reported as a check. Conclusions did not change between them in this run.
2. Network Dice in item 1 is the median over the hearts entering the ratio summaries; the median over all 50 hearts is also given (it reproduces the 0.768 for ukbb_cardiac in v02).
3. Item 4 uses complete hearts so every subset of a given k is defined on the same hearts.
4. Ties in the item 2 maximum count credit every tied network.
