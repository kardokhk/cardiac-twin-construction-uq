Project root `/project/home/p201509/work/cvri/simulation`. Written 2026-10-28 after the full arms

## B1. Smooth-surface fast layer (U8), declared sensitivity

- Layer: all myocardial elements whose centroid lies within 1.5 mm of the smooth LV or RV endocardial surface. The
  surface is the zero level set of a signed distance field of the blood pool, built from the label mask upsampled to
  0.25 mm with Gaussian smoothing (σ = one native voxel) and marching cubes. Cavity side only, as in A2.
- Unit test before any heart run: the A2 test file cases (plane, sphere r 25 mm, ellipsoid (20, 25, 40) mm in 8
  poses, at 1.5 and 1.0 mm voxels). Acceptance unchanged: ellipsoid layer volume within ±15% of the analytic shell and
  the 1.0/1.5 ratio within ±15%. The result is reported against the line whatever it is; the full run goes ahead
  either way.
- Scope: all 50 hearts, all primary constructions at both resolutions, RV wall 3 mm, 3 θ* per heart, both losses,
  same emulator design, refinement rule, seeds and prior as arm C.
- Reported: E1 CV for this layer, paired difference from primary (hearts resampled jointly), layer share of
  myocardial volume. The primary estimand stays on A2.

## B2. Heart × source variance model (U9), declared secondary model

- log(θ̂/θ*) ~ S × M + θ* + (1 | heart) + (1 | heart:source), REML, non-reference primary rows (the reference rows
  are fixed near 0 by construction and are left out). Both losses.
- Reported: variance components with cluster-bootstrap 95% CIs over hearts (2,000 resamples, seed 20261028), and the
  within-heart construction share (heart:source + residual attributable to S and M). Labelled secondary; E2 stays the
  pre-registered model.

## B3. RV wall lower bracket (U10)

- The 1.0 mm RV wall 2 mm set (10 hearts) is reported as a stated substitute for the unmeshable 1.5 mm level, labelled
  "not pre-registered; 1.5 mm level not estimable (49 of 50 fail G2 F3)". No new runs.

## B4. Extended noise grids (U11), declared extension of E3

- White and 40 Hz band-limited noise: add 5, 0, −5, −10 and −15 dB SNR to the existing 10 to 30 dB.
- Baseline wander: add 40% and 80% of lead QRS peak-to-peak.
- Electrode displacement: add 30 and 40 mm SD.
- 20 replicates per new level, same comparator definitions, seeds and emulators as A3. Both losses.
- Break-even rule as A3 on the extended grid; still "beyond grid" if outside it. The E3x extrapolation is then compared
  with the measured value and the difference reported.
